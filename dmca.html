DMCA Policy

Strict adherence to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act.

1. No Content Hosting on Our Servers

It is imperative to understand that IG Core is strictly an informational and utility tool that parses public links. We do not host, store, cache, or upload any copyrighted media files (videos, photos, audio, or reels) on our internal servers. Our technology simply provides an interface that allows users to download public content directly from Instagram's Content Delivery Network (CDN) servers via their own web browsers.

2. Respect for Copyright Ownership

We respect the intellectual property rights of all content creators, artists, and production companies, and we expect our users to do exactly the same. We do not encourage, support, or promote the piracy or unauthorized distribution of copyrighted content. Users are solely responsible for ensuring they possess the legal right, explicit permission, or fair-use justification to download and utilize the content they fetch via our tool.

3. Takedown Requests & Filing a Notice

Because IG Core does not host any files, we are physically unable to delete or remove content from the internet. If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent thereof, and you wish to have a specific video or photo removed, you must submit your DMCA takedown request directly to Instagram (Meta Platforms, Inc.). Once the content is removed from Instagram's primary servers, it will automatically and instantly become completely inaccessible through our tool.

However, if you believe that our tool is indexing a link inappropriately, you may contact us with a formal notice containing:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact URL generated by our tool that you wish to be blacklisted.
  • Your contact information (email address and phone number).

4. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable laws, IG Core has adopted a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion, users or IP addresses who are deemed to be repeat infringers or who repeatedly utilize our service for malicious or copyright-infringing purposes.
