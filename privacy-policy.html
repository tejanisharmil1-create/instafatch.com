Privacy Policy

Comprehensive guide on how we handle your data.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to IG Core. At our website, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This comprehensive privacy policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by IG Core and how it is utilized. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy while providing a fast and efficient tool for downloading public media.

2. Information We Collect (Log Files)

Like many other highly functional Web sites, IG Core makes use of log files. The information contained inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, the type of browser used, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring and exit pages, and potentially the number of clicks. This information is used strictly to analyze trends, administer the site, track user movement around the site, and gather broad demographic information. Importantly, IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

IG Core utilizes cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, record user-specific information regarding which pages the user accesses or visits, and customize web page content based on visitors' browser types or other information that the visitor sends via their browser. This ensures a smoother, faster, and more personalized user experience.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

  • Google, acting as a third-party advertisement vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on IG Core.
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve targeted ads to our users based on their visit to IG Core and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy directly at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Info)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among other rights, California consumers have the right to request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers. Furthermore, users have the right to request the deletion of their personal data. IG Core does not sell any personal data to third parties.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, the right to rectification, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to object to processing, and the right to data portability. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. Please contact us if you wish to exercise any of these rights.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. IG Core does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
