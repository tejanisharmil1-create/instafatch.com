Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to InstaFetch. We respect your personal privacy and are fully committed to protecting any data that might interact with our platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how log data, cookies, and network tracking elements are utilized to optimize server responses and support ad networks like Google AdSense.

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

InstaFetch functions as an on-the-fly client conduit. We do not store, cache, save, or mirror any media files, download history, or account credentials on our infrastructure. All files processed through our platform are fetched directly from public Content Delivery Network (CDN) servers to your local storage device.

2. Standard Log Files

Like almost all professional web portals, InstaFetch utilizes standard server log files. The data within these log logs includes IP addresses, browser types, ISPs, and timestamps. This data is collected purely for infrastructure health monitoring and is not linked to any personally identifiable credentials.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

To keep this application free, we integrate Google AdSense. Google utilizes specialized tracking mechanics (such as DART cookies) to understand your browsing tendencies across the web and deliver contextually relevant advertisements. You can manage these cookies via your browser settings.

4. External Connections

Our downloader interfaces directly with external public links. We hold no operational oversight regarding the cookie mechanisms or data collection policies of external third-party platforms.
